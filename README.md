# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
**1. Project Title / Headline**
   
**Super Store Sales Performance Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales, profit, customer segments, shipping modes, and regional performance for a retail superstore business.

**2. Short Description / Purpose**

The Super Store Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of retail performance by tracking key metrics such as sales, profit, quantity, delivery time, and regional trends. It enables business stakeholders to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions efficiently.

**3. Tech Stack**

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

Power BI Desktop – Primary tool for creating interactive dashboards and reports

Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Created calculated measures such as total sales, profit, average delivery time, and YoY comparisons

Data Modeling – Relationships between sales, customer, product, and geography data for accurate analysis

File Formats – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview

**4. Data Source**

 Super Store Dataset

The dataset contains transactional retail data including:

Order details (sales, profit, quantity)

Customer segments

Shipping modes

Product categories and sub-categories

Regional and state-level geography

Time-based attributes for monthly and yearly analysis

This structured data enables both trend analysis and comparative performance evaluation.

**5. Features / Highlights**

Retail businesses often struggle to track performance across multiple dimensions such as regions, product categories, shipping modes, and time periods. Raw transactional data makes it difficult to quickly identify profit drivers, loss-making areas, and customer behavior patterns.

**Goal of the Dashboard**

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

Monitor overall sales, profit, and quantity performance

Compare monthly sales and profit trends year-over-year

Analyze customer segments, payment modes, and shipping methods

Identify top-performing categories, sub-categories, and regions

Enable quick, interactive decision-making for business stakeholders

**Walkthrough of Key Visuals**

Key KPIs (Top Section)

Total Sales: 1.6M

Total Quantity Sold: 22K

Total Profit: 175K

Average Delivery Time: 4 days

Regional Filter (Top Center)

Interactive slicers for Central, East, South, and West regions to dynamically filter all visuals.

Sales by Segment (Donut Chart)

Distribution of sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

Sales by Payment Mode (Donut Chart)

Analysis of customer payment preferences such as COD, Online, and Cards.

Sales by Ship Mode (Bar & Donut Charts)

Comparison of sales across Standard, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping modes.

Monthly Sales by YoY (Line Chart)

Month-wise comparison of sales trends for 2019 vs 2020, highlighting seasonal patterns.

Monthly Profit by YoY (Line Chart)

Visual comparison of profit performance across months and years.

Sales by Category (Bar Chart)

Sales contribution from Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture.

Sales by Sub-Category (Bar Chart)

Top sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs, and Binders.

Sales & Profit by State (Map Visual)

Geographic visualization showing state-wise sales and profit distribution across the U.S.
